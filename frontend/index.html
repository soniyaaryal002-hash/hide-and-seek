    How the game works

    Rules
    View current game rules
    1. Everyone joins the same shared room code, and the app remembers their name, team, and room when the page refreshes.
    2. Before a round starts, everyone can see everyone else on the map.
    3. The admin sets the shared GPS zone centre, and the boundary starts at 600 m.
    4. Once the round starts, each team sees its own members by default, but the full room is visible for 30 seconds during each scheduled reveal window.
    5. Every 5 minutes, all player locations are visible for 30 seconds so the room can track movement.
    6. The zone shrinks gradually as the round clock runs down, and a fresh boundary is reset after each round ends.
    7. Photo clues, ping alerts, and room updates are broadcast to everyone currently in the shared room.
